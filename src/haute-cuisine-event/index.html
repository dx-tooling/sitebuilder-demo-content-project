FAQ

Can I attend solo?

Absolutely. We’ll seat you comfortably—many guests enjoy the chef’s-table format on their own.

Is there a vegetarian option?

Yes. Tell us in advance and we’ll tailor the tasting while preserving the structure of the menu.

Do you accommodate allergies?

We’ll do our best with advance notice. If you have severe allergies, include details so we can confirm feasibility.

What about cancellations?

If plans change, let us know as early as possible—seating is limited and we keep a waitlist.
